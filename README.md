## ⭐ Featured Projects

### 🛡️ Awaaz Shield
Real-time scam-call detection prototype that turns speech into a live transcript and scores it for financial-fraud signals: fake bank staff, KYC threats, OTP theft, courier impersonation, and digital-arrest intimidation.

- 🎙️ Live transcription from the browser mic or a tab, with risk scored cumulatively across the call
- 🌐 Heuristic scoring for English, Hindi, and Hinglish, with optional Claude-based scoring
- 🚨 Warnings to both parties on high risk, auto call termination, and escalation to operators on a live dashboard
- 📞 Consent-gated in-app voice calls, History and Settings screens, and optional Firebase auth and Firestore persistence
- ⚙️ **Built with:** Python, FastAPI, WebSockets, Deepgram, React, Vite

[GitHub](https://github.com/Arihantdev/awaaz-shield)
